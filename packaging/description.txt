PowerPaint is a native port of JS Paint, Isaiah Odhner's web remake of MS Paint, written in C++ against the Haiku API: no browser, no web runtime. It keeps JS Paint's layout, tools and sizes, and follows Haiku's conventions for menus, shortcuts and alerts.

All sixteen MS Paint tools with their options, selections you can move, resize, copy and smear, flip, rotate, stretch and skew, invert colours, the 28-colour palette with palette files, a text tool with a Fonts toolbar, zoom up to 1000% with a pixel grid, undo and redo. Pictures open and save in every format the system's translators handle.
